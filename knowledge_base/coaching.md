# Peak Form Coaching: demo knowledge base (made-up business)

## [about] About Peak Form Coaching
Peak Form Coaching is an online fitness and nutrition coaching business. Coaching runs through our app with weekly check-ins with your coach. We work with beginners and experienced lifters, aged 18 and over.

## [programs] Coaching programs
We offer three programs: Starter (12 weeks, training plan plus monthly check-ins), Transform (12 weeks, training and nutrition plan plus weekly check-ins) and Elite (6 months, everything in Transform plus two video calls a month).

## [pricing] Prices
Starter costs 99 USD per month. Transform costs 179 USD per month. Elite costs 299 USD per month. Prices include all plan updates. There is no sign-up fee.

## [trial] Free consultation
Everyone starts with a free 20-minute consultation call with a coach. It is not a sales pitch: the coach checks your goals, training history and any injuries, then recommends a program. Book it with the "Book a free call" button.

## [cancel] Cancelling
You can cancel any time before your next monthly payment from the app's Account page. Cancelling stops future payments; the current month runs to its end.

## [equipment] Equipment needed
Plans are built around what you have: a full gym, a home setup with dumbbells, or bodyweight only. Tell your coach on the consultation call.

## [nutrition] Nutrition plans
Transform and Elite include a flexible nutrition plan with calorie and protein targets and a simple meal guide. We do not sell supplements and we never require them.

## [results] Results and timelines
Most clients notice strength and energy changes in 3 to 4 weeks and visible changes in 8 to 12 weeks if they follow the plan. Results vary and are not guaranteed.

## [health] Injuries and medical conditions
We are not doctors. If you have an injury, are pregnant or have a medical condition, get your doctor's clearance first and tell your coach; the plan is adapted around it.

## [hours] Coach response times
Coaches reply to app messages within one working day, Sunday to Thursday, 9am to 6pm Gulf time.
